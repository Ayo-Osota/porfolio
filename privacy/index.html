Privacy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is what happens to your information, in plain words.

Last updated 16 October 2026.

What I collect

Nothing, unless you email me. This site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

If you email hello@ayoosota.com, I get your email address, your name if it is in the message, and whatever you choose to write.

How I use it

I use your email only to reply to you and to run any work we agree on. I do not sell it, rent it or share it for advertising. I do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

Other companies that see a little

When a page loads, your browser asks a few other companies for files. They can see your internet address and your browser type, as with most websites.

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts.
  • jsDelivr supplies the small script that draws the icons.
  • My hosting provider keeps basic server records, such as the time of a visit and the page asked for.

I do not control how these companies handle that information. Each has its own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to LinkedIn, X, TikTok, my resume on Notion and a few project sites. They have their own privacy rules, and I am not responsible for them.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have kept from your emails, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email hello@ayoosota.com and I will deal with it. If you are in Canada, you also have rights under the federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Changes

If this changes, for example if I add a form or analytics, I will update this page and the date at the top.

What I build

I use AI to take the boring, repeat jobs off your plate. Here is what that looks like in real life.

  1. Sound familiar?

    A customer asks about your service on Friday evening. You reply on Monday, and they have already hired someone else.

    What I set up

    Every new enquiry gets a reply within minutes, even at 9pm. If they go quiet, they get a friendly nudge. They can book a time with you straight from the message.

    No enquiry gets forgotten, and more of them become customers.

    Your team keeps asking you what to do next, or waits until you chase them.

How it works

Five steps. About eight weeks until I hand it over, then 30 days of support. It takes 2 to 3 hours a week of your time while I build.

  1. Week 1

    Discovery and audit

    We start with a 90-minute call. I list every repeat job in your business, look at the tools you use, and work out where you sit on the Sanity Quadrant. You get a written Sanity Audit Report: what I found, what I would fix, and a fixed price.

    From you: Access to your tools, 2 to 3 hours of your time, and honest notes on how things work today.

  2. Week 2

    Design

    I draw up a plan, in plain language, for how each job will run by itself. Nothing gets built until you say yes.

    From you: Your OK on the plan, and answers on anything that is unclear.

  3. Weeks 3 to 6

    Build

    I build it and connect it to the tools you already use, like your email, calendar and customer list. I add the AI parts, such as replies to new enquiries and short report summaries, plus one live screen that shows how the business is doing. I test it against 20 or more real situations before it goes live.

    From you: Access to your accounts, and quick answers when I ask (within a day or two).

  4. Weeks 7 to 8

    Train and hand off

    A live walkthrough of 2 to 3 hours with your team. Simple guides and step-by-step pictures, a list of common problems and fixes, full admin access handed to you, and a checklist you tick off yourself.

    From you: Your team in the room.

  5. 30 days

    Support

    If anything breaks, I fix it for free. I also make small tweaks, help by email or Slack, and we check in with a call on day 30.

    From you: Your questions.

You end up with a system you can run without me.
